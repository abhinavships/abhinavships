### What I do

- **Grounded AI agents.** Copilots and browser agents that cite their evidence, verify every number before showing it, and refuse unsafe or unprovable actions.
- **Evaluation systems.** Golden and synthetic test sets, deterministic scorers, fault injection to test the tests, and mutation testing for UIs.
- **Backends that survive failure.** Postgres-native concurrency, idempotent event handling, leases and self-healing workers.

Open to **AI engineering** and **backend engineering** roles.
